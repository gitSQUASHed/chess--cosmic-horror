# T-13 Phase 2 king movement and jump-dash

## Scope
- Free movement on pillar surfaces using a physics body.
- Jump-dash: fast burst with limited turn rate, can cross narrow gaps or land in one.
- Falling into a hole or void triggers the VOID ending. Walls block movement.
- Camera follows the king (T-03).

## Acceptance criteria
- A dash crosses a one-tile gap and fails across a wide gap.
- Steering during a dash is limited to a slow turn.
