# T-11 Phase 2 king movement and jump-dash

## Scope
- Free movement on solid pillars.
- Jump-dash: fast burst, slow turning only, can cross narrow gaps or end in one.
- Falling into a hole triggers the VOID ending; walls block movement.
- Facing is shown on the placeholder label.

## Acceptance criteria
- A dash crosses a one-tile gap and fails across a wide one.
- Steering during a dash is limited to a slow turn.
