# Endings

## 1. Captured by a hand

Trigger: a hand catches the king.

Result: game-over scene. A speech bubble or distant line from the players' origin says that they can finally go downstairs.

## 2. Lost to the void

Trigger: the king falls into a gap or off the world.

Result: the screen becomes completely dark. Text states, in essence:

> “Where did the king go? Anyway, let’s go downstairs.”

## 3. Escape to the other player

Trigger: the king travels about 500 tiles toward the side encoded by the Phase 1 clock, reaches the edge, and jumps down.

Result: a cutscene shows the king dropping onto the chess clock and switching to the other player. The king says:

> “Now it’s my turn.”

The initial king colour determines which side is correct. The precise mapping between colour and side is still to be set.

## 4. Endurance

Trigger: survive Phase 2 for ten minutes.

Result: text appears:

> “Yeah, let’s just clean up later.”

This outcome should be extremely difficult but possible.
